About this prototype

The content comes from From Closed to Open: Practices and Guidelines for Open Research at Ulster University by Hanna, L., Evans, E., Bond, R., Butter, S., Cahoon, A., Casey, F., McChesney, I. and Shukla, P. (2026), developed by the Open & Responsible Research Workstream and licensed CC BY 4.0. It was inspired by the University of Surrey Open Research Checklist and CONUL's Navigating open research.

The guide and its checklist are combined into one page here. Each action has been given a short title, a one-line reason, an effort estimate and an "applies if" tag. These labels are draft tagging for illustration only; the workstream would need to confirm which items are genuinely required by policy or funders.

Missing a practice? The guide asks for suggestions via the workstream lead. For examples from colleagues, see the Ulster open research case studies.
